# Gandha-Siri: The Sandalwood Farmer's Guard 🌳🛡️

**Gandha-Siri** is a comprehensive digital asset management and security application designed specifically for Sandalwood (Srigandha) farmers. Developed as part of the MindMatrix VTU Internship Program, the app empowers small farmers to cultivate high-value timber with confidence by providing digital traceability, security alerts, and legal guidance.

---

## 🌟 The Vision
Sandalwood is a part of our heritage, but farmers often fear theft and are confused by legal procedures. **Gandha-Siri** acts as a "Digital Guard," bridging the gap between cultivation and legal harvesting through technology.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 1. 📋 Tree Register (Digital Asset Ledger)
* **Unique Tree ID:** Automatically generates a unique identifier for every tree registered.
* **Photo & GPS Tagging:** Capture high-resolution photos and precise GPS coordinates using `FusedLocationProviderClient`.
* **Girth Tracking:** Record trunk measurements to monitor biological growth.

### 2. 📈 Growth Tracker & Analytics
* **Visual History:** View a Line Chart of each tree's girth progress over time.
* **Wealth Analytics:** A dashboard-level graph showing the projected growth of your total natural wealth.
* **Maturity Gauge:** A simulated engine that calculates the heartwood maturity percentage.

### 3. 🚨 Estate Security Center
* **Panic Button:** A one-tap emergency system that sends a simulated SMS and system notification with GPS coordinates to neighbors and authorities.
* **Security Audit:** A checklist for farmers to verify boundary fencing, lighting, and motion sensors.
* **Geo-Fencing Status:** Real-time monitoring of the farm's security perimeter.

### 4. ⚖️ Legal & Ownership Tools
* **Official Digital Certificate:** Generates a formal "Proof of Cultivation" record including Tree ID, GPS, and date for Forest Department verification.
* **Legal Guide:** Up-to-date information on the **Karnataka Sandalwood Policy 2022**, ownership rights, and harvest procedures.
* **Harvest Countdown:** Estimated time remaining until the tree is ready for commercial use.

### 5. ✨ GenAI Tree Advisor (Simulated)
* **Vision Scan:** Uses a simulated AI vision engine to analyze tree texture and provide customized cultivation advice based on growth stages (Sapling vs. Mature).

## 🛠️ Technical Implementation
* **Language:** 100% Kotlin (Modern Android Development).
* **Architecture:** MVVM (Model-View-ViewModel) for clean, maintainable code.
* **Database:** **Room DB** for secure, persistent local storage of tree logs.
* **Maps:** **Google Maps SDK** (Satellite View) with lite-mode previews on the dashboard.
* **UI/UX:** A professional **"Sandal/Wood"** color palette with full **Bilingual Support (English & Kannada)**.
